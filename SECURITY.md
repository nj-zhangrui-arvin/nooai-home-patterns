# Security

## 中文

- 不要在 Issue、PR、示例或测试中提交真实家庭网络、实体 ID、地址、人物位置、截图、token、密钥、cookie、日志或备份。
- 如果公开内容可能被用来访问或推断真实家庭环境，请只通过私密渠道报告影响，不要粘贴敏感值。
- `nooai-home-patterns` 的输出是参考实现，不应直接绕过本地物理安全门禁去控制有安全影响的设备。

## English

- Do not submit real home networks, entity IDs, addresses, person locations, screenshots, tokens, keys, cookies, logs, or backups in public issues, PRs, examples, or tests.
- If public content may reveal or grant access to a real household, report the impact privately and do not paste the sensitive value.
- `nooai-home-patterns` is a reference implementation and should not bypass local physical safety gates for safety-critical automations.
